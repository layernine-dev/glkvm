GLKVM Clean

Connections

Open each device in its own frameless window.

Use a LAN or VPN address. Passwords are encrypted using your macOS keychain. Leave a saved password blank to keep it; changing the address clears it. Saving a password retries sign-in in the background.

Controls

Choose how your device windows behave.

Keyboard shortcuts

App settings
⌘,
Open / close device settings
⌘⇧O
Enable / disable remote control
⌘⇧I
Move window mode
⌘⇧M
Open a connection
⌘1 … ⌘9
Reload device
⌘R

In move mode, drag the video to reposition the window. Press ⌘⇧M again to resume control. In view-only mode, you can always drag the video.

Device settings open in a separate window. The shared KVM window always stays clean. Camera, microphone and downloads remain unavailable.
